# Tier 1: The Literal Assistant

## instruction
> "Make me breakfast."

A human understands this from context. A literal machine cannot, because it does not know what food, where it is, how much, how to cook it, or when to stop.

## Rewritten instruction (for a completely literal machine)

**Goal:** Prepare one serving of French toast, 2 sausages, scrambled eggs, sliced strawberries, and 1 cup of Arabian tea, and place it on the dining table.


### Part A: Check before starting

**Step 1:** Stand at the kitchen counter next to the stove.

**Step 2:** Check that all stove dials are on "Off". If any dial is not on "Off", turn it to "Off".

**Step 3:** Open the fridge. Check that you have 3 eggs, at least 1/3 cup of milk, butter, 2 sausages, 5 strawberries, and 2 mint leaves.

**Step 4:** Open the pantry. Check that you have at least 2 slices of bread, sugar, and 1 black tea bag.

**Step 5:** Open the spice cupboard. Check that you have cinnamon, 1 cardamom pod, salt, and black pepper.

**Step 6:** If any item is missing, stop. Report which item is missing. Do not replace it with something else.


### Part B: Gather everything

**Step 7:** Take all the items from Steps 3 to 5 and place them on the counter.

**Step 8:** Place these on the counter: 1 frying pan, 2 mixing bowls (one shallow, one small), 1 fork, 1 spatula, 1 pair of tongs, 1 cutting board, 1 knife, 1 dinner plate, 1 mug, 1 spoon, 1 roll of paper towel.

### Part C: Strawberries

**Step 9:** Rinse the 5 strawberries under cold running water for 10 seconds.

**Step 10:** Place them on the cutting board. Cut off the green tops and throw the tops in the trash bin.

**Step 11:** Cut each strawberry into 4 slices. Place the slices in a clean bowl and set it aside.

### Part D: Tea

**Step 12:** Fill the kettle with 1 cup (240 ml) of water and turn it on. Wait until it switches off (the water is boiling).

**Step 13:** Place the cardamom pod on the cutting board. Press it with the flat side of the knife until it splits open.

**Step 14:** Put the cracked cardamom, 1 tea bag, 2 mint leaves, and 1 teaspoon of sugar into the mug.

**Step 15:** Pour the boiling water into the mug. Set a timer for 4 minutes.

**Step 16:** When the timer ends, remove the tea bag, cardamom, and mint with the spoon and put them in the trash. Taste the tea. If it is not sweet enough, add 1 more teaspoon of sugar and stir for 5 seconds. Set the mug aside.

### Part E: Sausages

**Step 17:** Place the frying pan on the front burner. Turn the dial to medium (number 5 of 10). Wait 2 minutes.

**Step 18:** Place the 2 sausages in the pan using the tongs.

**Step 19:** Cook for 8 to 10 minutes. Turn them every 2 minutes.

**Step 20:** The sausages are done when they are brown on all sides and have no pink inside when one is cut open. If pink remains, cook 2 more minutes and check again.

**Step 21:** Move the sausages onto the dinner plate with the tongs.

**Step 22:** Turn the burner dial to "Off". Wipe the pan with a paper towel held in the tongs. Throw the towel in the trash.

### Part F: French toast

**Step 23:** Crack 1 egg on the edge of the shallow bowl and open it over the bowl. Put the shell in the trash. If any shell falls in, remove it with the spoon.

**Step 24:** Add 1/4 cup of milk and 1/2 teaspoon of cinnamon. Beat with the fork for 30 seconds until fully mixed.

**Step 25:** Turn the burner to medium (5). Place the pan on it. Add 1 tablespoon of butter and wait until it has fully melted (about 30 seconds).

**Step 26:** Dip 1 slice of bread in the mixture for 5 seconds on each side. Place it in the pan. Repeat with the second slice.

**Step 27:** Cook for 2 to 3 minutes. Lift one corner with the spatula. When the underside is golden brown, flip it. Cook 2 to 3 minutes more on the other side.

**Step 28:** Move both slices onto the plate next to the sausages.

**Step 29:** Turn the burner to "Off". Wipe the pan as in Step 22.

### Part G: Scrambled eggs

**Step 30:** Crack 2 eggs into the small bowl, one at a time, as in Step 23.

**Step 31:** Add 1 tablespoon of milk, 1 pinch of salt, and 1 pinch of black pepper. Beat with the fork for 30 seconds.

**Step 32:** Turn the burner to medium-low (number 3 of 10). Place the pan on it. Add 1 teaspoon of butter and wait 20 seconds until melted.

**Step 33:** Pour the egg mixture into the pan.

**Step 34:** Every 10 seconds, push the eggs from the edge to the center with the spatula.

**Step 35:** The eggs are done when they form soft, shiny lumps with no liquid egg left at the bottom of the pan. This takes about 2 to 3 minutes.

**Step 36:** Turn the burner to "Off". Move the eggs onto the plate right away. They keep cooking from the heat for about 15 seconds.

### Part H: Serve

**Step 37:** Place the strawberry slices on the plate, beside the French toast.

**Step 38:** Place the dinner plate on the dining table with the fork on its left and the knife on its right.

**Step 39:** Place the mug of tea to the upper right of the plate.

### Part I: Finish

**Step 40:** The task is complete only when all of these are true: the plate and mug are on the table, all stove dials are on "Off", and the kettle is switched off.

**Step 41:** Report: "Breakfast is ready."

### If something goes wrong

- If a burner will not light or heat, turn the dial to "Off", stop, and report "Stove not working."
- If the food burns or smokes, turn the burner to "Off", move the pan off the heat, stop, and report.
- If an ingredient is missing, stop and report which one (see Step 6).

## Reasoning Challenge: Which parts of my original instruction required assumptions?

"Make me breakfast" assumed that the assistant already knew:

- **What** breakfast I wanted (which foods and drinks)
- **Where** the ingredients and equipment were kept
- **How much** of each food to prepare
- **Which equipment** to use and how to use it (for example, what "medium heat" means)
- **How** to prepare each item and in what order
- **How to tell when** each item was done (for example, eggs with no liquid left)
- **Where** to place the finished meal
- **When to stop** (what counts as "finished")
- **What to do** if something goes wrong, such as a missing ingredient or a stove that will not light



A human fills in all of this from experience and context. A computer cannot. Every one of these details has to be written as an explicit instruction, or the machine will fail or do the wrong thing.
